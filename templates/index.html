Tips for Better Recognition

  • Enable the camera and check your framing so your upper body and hands are clearly visible.
  • Start from a neutral, hands-down position; a short countdown will begin before recording.
  • Perform the sign clearly within the 3-second recording window while watching the progress bar.
  • Use the Cancel button or press Esc if you’re not ready or make a mistake; press Space to start quickly.
  • Ensure good lighting and minimize background distractions for better results.
